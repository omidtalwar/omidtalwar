# Privacy Policy for MoneyGo **Effective Date: October 3, 2026** **Last Updated: October 3, 2026** MoneyGo ("MoneyGo", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how MoneyGo accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MoneyGo mobile application. MoneyGo is a personal and small-business record-keeping application designed to help users organize income, expenses, customer and supplier balances, debts, shopping records, and other financial records. MoneyGo is not a bank, lender, payment processor, investment service, or financial advisory service. ## 1. Information You Enter Into MoneyGo Depending on how you use the app, you may enter information such as: - Income and expense amounts - Transaction dates - Transaction categories - Transaction descriptions and notes - Customer names - Supplier names - Customer and supplier balances - Money lent or borrowed - Cash sales and purchases - Business expenses - Shopping-list items and estimated costs - Categories and tags - Currency preferences - Receipt images or other images you choose to attach to transactions - Other financial records that you voluntarily enter into the app This information is used only to provide MoneyGo's record-keeping, reporting, backup, restore, search, organization, and related features. ## 2. Information Stored on Your Device MoneyGo is designed to work offline. Your transaction records and other locally created information may be stored directly on your device so that MoneyGo can operate without a continuous internet connection. MoneyGo does not require your locally stored records to be transmitted to us simply because you create or edit a transaction. If you uninstall the application, clear the application's storage, reset your device, or otherwise delete locally stored information without creating a backup, that information may be permanently lost. ## 3. Receipt Images and Files MoneyGo may allow you to select or attach receipt images and other supporting files to your records. MoneyGo accesses these files only when necessary to provide the feature you request, such as attaching a receipt to an expense record. Files you select are not sold to advertisers or data brokers. Where supported by the operating system, MoneyGo attempts to request only the access necessary for the specific file or image selected by you. ## 4. Optional Cloud Backup MoneyGo may provide an optional cloud backup and restore feature. Cloud backup is not required for MoneyGo's basic offline record-keeping features. If you choose to enable cloud backup, information included in your backup may be transmitted over the internet and stored with the cloud storage provider used by MoneyGo. **Cloud backup provider:Firebase ** Depending on the backup configuration, backup information may include your MoneyGo records, categories, transaction information, customer or supplier information, settings, and attachments included in the backup. We do not use cloud backup data for advertising or to build advertising profiles. Please also review the privacy policy of the cloud storage provider used by MoneyGo because information processed by that provider is subject to its applicable privacy practices. ## 5. Backup Export and Import MoneyGo may allow you to create offline backup files and restore records from those files. Where the application offers password-protected backup files, the password is used to help protect the contents of the backup. You are responsible for keeping exported backup files and their passwords secure. If you manually share, upload, copy, or store an exported MoneyGo backup using another service, the privacy practices of that external service will apply. ## 6. Data We Do Not Sell MoneyGo does not sell your personal information or financial records. We do not sell transaction histories, customer records, supplier records, receipt images, debt records, or shopping records to advertisers or data brokers. ## 7. Financial Information The financial information stored in MoneyGo is provided by you for personal record keeping. MoneyGo does not: - Hold or deposit your money - Provide bank accounts - Issue loans - Process payments - Transfer money - Provide credit - Trade stocks or cryptocurrency - Provide investment services - Provide personalized financial advice MoneyGo should not be treated as a bank statement, financial institution, accounting certification, or professional financial advisory service. ## 8. App Permissions MoneyGo may request device permissions when necessary to provide specific features. Depending on your device and the features you use, these permissions may include access needed to: - Select receipt images or documents - Save or export backup files - Import backup files - Send reminders or notifications MoneyGo uses permissions only for the functionality for which they are requested. You may revoke applicable permissions through your device settings, although doing so may prevent certain features from working. ## 9. Notifications and Reminders If you enable reminders, MoneyGo may send local notifications to remind you to record or review your financial activity. Notification preferences can be controlled within MoneyGo or through your device's system settings. ## 10. Data Sharing We do not share your MoneyGo financial records with unrelated third parties for advertising or marketing. Information may be processed by service providers only where necessary to provide functionality that you choose to use, such as optional cloud backup. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request. If additional third-party services are added to MoneyGo in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. ## 11. Analytics and Advertising **MoneyGo currently does not use advertising SDKs or sell user data for advertising purposes.** **[IMPORTANT: If MoneyGo uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, AppLovin, or another analytics/advertising SDK, this section must be updated before publication.]** ## 12. Data Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled through MoneyGo. MoneyGo's offline-first design helps limit unnecessary transmission of your financial records. When information is transmitted for an online feature such as cloud backup, we use the security mechanisms provided by the application, operating system, and applicable service provider. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device with an appropriate password, PIN, fingerprint, or other device-security mechanism. ## 13. Data Retention Records stored locally in MoneyGo remain on your device until you: - Delete individual records; - Delete applicable MoneyGo data; - Clear the application's data; - Uninstall the application; or - Otherwise remove the information from your device. Cloud backups, if enabled, may remain available until you delete them or until they are deleted according to the retention practices of the applicable cloud storage service. Exported backup files remain wherever you choose to store them until you delete them. ## 14. Data Deletion MoneyGo allows users to control records stored locally in the application. You can delete individual transactions and other applicable records from within MoneyGo. You may also remove locally stored application data using your device settings or by uninstalling MoneyGo. If cloud backup is enabled, deletion of local information may not automatically delete previously created cloud backups. Cloud backup copies should be deleted through the applicable backup feature or cloud storage service. For questions or assistance relating to privacy or deletion, contact us using the contact information below. ## 15. Children's Privacy MoneyGo is designed as a general financial record-keeping utility and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly use MoneyGo to collect personal information from children for advertising or behavioral profiling purposes. If you believe that information relating to a child has been handled improperly, please contact us. ## 16. International Users MoneyGo may be used in different countries and supports multiple currencies and languages. When you choose to use an online service, such as cloud backup, information may be processed in countries where the applicable service provider operates. ## 17. Third-Party Services Certain optional functionality may depend on third-party services. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of any external service you choose to use in connection with MoneyGo. ## 18. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when MoneyGo's features, technologies, legal requirements, or data practices change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this policy. We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically. ## 19.
